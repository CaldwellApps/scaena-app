What is Scaena?

Scaena is a premium, privacy-focused iOS and iPadOS tracking application. It allows you to explore trending TV shows and movies, organize your media watchlist, monitor upcoming show release dates, view airing schedules, and synchronize your watch history seamlessly—all protected behind fully native spoiler controls.

Is macOS supported?

Scaena is designed and optimized specifically for iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 18.0+). While Apple Silicon Macs can download iPad apps, Scaena is not officially supported or optimized for macOS and does not work well on desktop environments. We strongly recommend using Scaena on iPhone and iPad for the best experience.

Does Scaena track my personal data?

No. Scaena is built on a 100% local database model using Apple's secure Core Data container. Your settings and API keys sync exclusively via your personal Apple iCloud Key-Value store. No external servers or third-party tracking databases collect your personal data.

What languages does Scaena support?

Scaena is fully localized into 22 global languages: English, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, French, German, Portuguese (Portugal & Brazil), Italian, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Hindi, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, and Polish. You can customize the interface language directly in the app settings.

How does Scaena avoid TV show episode spoilers?

Scaena features a native "Hide Episode Spoilers" toggle. When enabled, episode descriptions are hidden behind a spoiler alert, and specific episode thumbnails are replaced with general season posters until you mark the episode as watched.

How can I share a list, and what are List Snapshots?

You can share any watchlist (Full, Aired/Released, or Upcoming), default status list (Watching, Plan to Watch, Completed, On Hold, Dropped), or personal Custom List (requires Scaena Pro) directly from Scaena:

  • One-Tap Web Sharing: Tap the Share button at the top of any watchlist, default list, or custom list view to generate a secure, interactive web link (caldwellappsco.com/list/...). Anyone opening the link can view your list in any browser on desktop or mobile, search and filter items, or tap Open in Scaena to import the entire list into their own app. (Note: Sharing personal Custom Lists requires Scaena Pro, as custom list creation is a Pro feature).
  • Immutable List Snapshots: Whenever you share a list, Scaena saves an immutable snapshot captured at that exact moment in time under Settings → List Snapshots. The snapshot records the title, filter criteria, item count, preview artwork, and full compressed payload.
  • CloudKit Link Revocation: You retain complete control over your shared links. If you ever want to unpublish a shared list, simply open the snapshot in Settings and tap Revoke Share. Scaena immediately registers the revocation in Apple's CloudKit Public Database, blocking web viewers from viewing the contents. You can un-revoke it at any time to restore access.
  • Search, Filter & Trash Recovery: Filter and sort your saved snapshots by category or media type, copy links anytime without re-exporting, and restore accidentally deleted snapshots from Recently Deleted within 30 days.

Which Simkl features does Scaena support on iOS?

Scaena integrates directly with Simkl's API to support the following platform features on iPhone and iPad:

  • 2-Way Synchronization: Background syncing for watch history, episode progress, ratings, and status lists (Watching, Plan to Watch, Completed, On Hold, Dropped).
  • Modern AUTH V2: Secure authentication using Simkl's modern OAuth with PKCE and automatic token refresh.
  • Multi-Session Rewatch Tracking: Full support for Simkl's rewatch engine, allowing you to log multiple rewatch cycles per title without overwriting original watch dates.
  • Simkl Custom Lists (Beta): Full 2-way read/write for Simkl custom lists (Simkl Pro/VIP)—create, edit, delete, favorite, sort by custom position, add private notes and tags, manage collaborators, and convert local iCloud lists to Simkl.
  • Explore & Trending Feeds: Live trending TV shows and movies sourced directly from Simkl, filterable by Today, This Week, This Month, In Theaters, and Certification.
  • Personalized Recommendations: Access personalized title recommendations tailored to your Simkl watch history (for Simkl Pro/VIP members).

How do I sync my watchlist with Simkl?

Simply open Settings in Scaena and connect your Simkl account. Scaena automatically synchronizes your watch history, watchlist progress, and ratings in the background, keeping your library up to date across all your devices.

How does Rewatch Tracking work with Simkl?

Scaena Pro allows you to log multiple rewatches for your favorite TV shows and movies without overwriting or losing your original watch history. You can track rewatch episode progress per session and sync seamlessly with Simkl Pro & VIP accounts.

How do I upgrade from Simkl AUTH V1 to AUTH V2?

Navigate to Settings → Simkl Account inside Scaena. If your account is linked using legacy AUTH V1, you will see an "Upgrade to AUTH V2" option. Tap it to re-authenticate using the modern, secure OAuth with PKCE flow. Please note that Simkl plans to deprecate AUTH V1 around April 2027, so we recommend upgrading to ensure continuous sync and improved security.

Can I customize the layout of TV Show and Movie detail pages?

Yes! Under Settings → Appearance → TV Show & Movie Pages, you can use native drag-and-drop handles to reorder sections (Show/Movie Info, Overview, List Status, My Rating, Cast, and Seasons) to suit your workflow across the entire app.

Can I choose which web browser opens external links?

Yes. Under Settings → General → Link Handling, you can select between the In-App Safari browser, your device's Default Browser, or "Always Ask" to choose dynamically from browsers installed on your device (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, DuckDuckGo, etc.).

How do I change the App Icon or visual theme?

Navigate to Settings → Appearance. Scaena Pro unlocks alternate App Icons (System, Light, and Dark), 9 curated visual themes (such as Dark Grey, Dark Blue, Arctic Night, Dark Cocoa, and Plum), custom accent color pickers, and high-density Compact Cards layouts.

What metadata services does Scaena use?

Scaena retrieves show schedules, user lists, and artwork from Simkl, along with high-resolution episode backdrops from TheTVDB (TVDB). All metadata is loaded automatically in the background.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Overview

Scaena is designed with user privacy in mind. We do not run any remote user tracking, analytics, or profiling servers. Your data belongs entirely to you.

2. Information We Process

We process credentials and metadata necessary to run the service:

  • Simkl API Credentials: Securely stored on-device in local Keychain or Core Data storage.
  • Media Watchlist & Rating Data: Retrieved and cached locally to display watchlists, release calendars, and recommendations.
  • Recommendations & Discovery: Title recommendations are retrieved via standard Simkl API requests using your client key or personal account token when authenticated.
  • App Store Purchases: Verification details are processed securely by Apple. Scaena does not collect or store your payment details.
  • System logs: Transient error logs kept locally for diagnostics.

3. Storage and Syncing

Your watchlist, lists, and settings are stored locally on your device. We utilize Apple iCloud Synchronization to securely backup and sync your settings across your own iOS/macOS devices. We do not have access to your iCloud container or your personal data.

4. Third-Party API Data Sharing

To synchronize your media watch history and deliver personalized show, movie, and anime recommendations, Scaena communicates directly with the Simkl API. When requesting artwork and metadata, the app communicates with Simkl and TheTVDB. Any information shared with these services is governed strictly by their respective privacy policies.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security protocols to transmit data over secure HTTPS connections. Your access token is kept in secure local storage or Keychain services.

6. Your Choices & Deletion

You can disconnect your Simkl account at any time via the Settings menu, which immediately deletes Simkl tokens and credentials stored inside the application database. Since all other data resides in iCloud, deleting the app will remove local storage caches.
